About the branch

Founded in 2003, the IEEE Student Branch at NAIST supports students through events that explore the edge of technology — while building strong international connections along the way.

Founded in 2003
A graduate student branch at NAIST
Part of IEEE Kansai Section
IEEE Japan Council · Region 10
Open to all NAIST students
Master's and doctoral
Mission
Be the bridge for learning the edge of technology to NAIST students

We aim to support students through our events to learn the edge of technology while having fun and making strong international connections!

Vision
Become an exemplary Graduate IEEE SB in Japan!

We want to bring inspiration and support not only to students from our school, but to those of other graduate schools in Japan! We believe that collaboration is the key!

What we do

Activities we run together with other Student Branches across the IEEE Kansai Section.

English Presentation Competition

Students across Kansai-section universities present their research to evaluate and sharpen their English communication.

Women in Engineering Symposium

Talks from female researchers and engineers, with space for women in research and students to connect.

Research Exchange Meeting

Students from universities across the Kansai section share research spanning different fields and campuses.

Brainstorming Competition

Teams practice and refine brainstorming techniques to tackle real problems together.
